## Icebreaker: Predict the Next Token

*Estimated time: 20 minutes.*

Today's first practice is **seeing where AI answers come from**. No accounts, no installs — just your team, paper, and the course's [Token Prediction Playground](https://www.billmongan.com/Ursinus-CS357-Fall2026/TokenPredictor), a tiny next-word model that runs entirely in your browser.

The goal is to feel the top row of the Capabilities and Limitations table — *Next Token Prediction* — from the inside. Feed it a common phrase and watch it get confident (capability); feed it rare or ungrounded words and watch it shrug (limitation).
